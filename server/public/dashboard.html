Welcome to Samanvay

Use this dashboard to get the approvals your project needs from the Land Revenue, Electricity and Pollution Control departments. Answer a few questions once. We work out which approvals you need and send your details to each department, so you do not have to fill in the same information again.

Electricity

If yes, MSEDCL (Mahavitaran) checks that enough power is available at your site.

100 kW or more needs a high-voltage connection and approval from a senior MSEDCL engineer.

Step 4 of 4

Environment and safety

If yes, you need permission from the Pollution Control Board (MPCB) before you build. If you are not sure, we include it to be safe.

If yes, you need a special hazardous-waste permission and a registered waste-disposal partner. If you are not sure, we include it to be safe.

What the statuses mean

Overall result

All approved
Every department has approved. You can go ahead.
Waiting for departments
At least one department is still working on your application (pending, site inspection or review).
Action needed from you
Something must be fixed before you can go ahead, for example a rejection, unpaid electricity bills, a claim on the land or a pollution-rule problem.
Identity check failed
The company PAN does not match the department records, so the check was stopped.

Each department

Not sent yet

Check completed
We received the department’s records. The line underneath shows what they say, for example Approved or Pending. It does not by itself mean approved.
Not checked
The department was not checked. The line underneath gives one of two different reasons:
  • Waiting for Stage 1 Pollution Control Board only: not sent yet, because Land and Electricity are not both approved. Nothing is wrong with the department.
  • Department system not responding We tried, but the department’s computer system did not answer. Try again later.

How outages are handled

1. Problems stay contained: If the Pollution Control Board’s system is down, the Land and Electricity checks still finish, with the status PARTIAL_SUCCESS.

2. Automatic pause: After 3 failures in a row, Samanvay pauses calls to that system (status OPEN) to protect the other systems.
