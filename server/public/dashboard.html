Resilience Architecture Rules

          

            
1. Failure Isolation: If MPCB experiences a timeout or 503 error, Land and Electricity verifications complete successfully with status PARTIAL_SUCCESS.

            
2. Circuit Breaker Trip: 3 consecutive failures trip the circuit to OPEN state, shielding downstream systems.
