Help and frequently asked questions

Do I fill in a separate form for each department?
No. You answer the questions once and Samanvay sends your details to each department that needs them. You still sign, pay fees and make the final submission on each department’s official website.
In what order are the departments checked?
Land and Electricity are checked at the same time. The Pollution Control Board check starts only after both have approved.
What do I need to log in?
The Aadhaar or PAN number you registered with. We send a one-time password (OTP) to your registered mobile number.
How do I track my application?
Log in and open “Track my application” on your dashboard.
