Maharashtra single-window service

One application.

Samanvay connects the Land Revenue, Electricity and Pollution Control departments. You fill in your project details once, and every department that needs them gets them. No more filling in the same form three times.

How it works

From application to approval, in one place

Instead of visiting three separate websites, you tell us about your project once. We send your details to each department that needs them.

You (citizen or business) Fill in one application
Samanvay Works out what you need and shares your details with each department
Stage 1 — checked at the same time

Land Revenue

7/12 land record, mutation and ownership check

Electricity (MSEDCL)

New power connection and power load approval

Stage 2 — after Land and Electricity are both approved

Pollution Control (MPCB)

Permission to set up your unit (Consent to Establish)

Approvals from every department All tracked in one place
Why Samanvay
